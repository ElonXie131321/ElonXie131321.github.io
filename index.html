01 / ABOUT 關於我

A little about
who I am.

A student, a storyteller, and a curious mind.
一位學生、說故事的人，也是持續探索的好奇者。

ENGLISH

Hello! I'm Xie-049, a third-year student studying Artificial Intelligence at Asia University in Taiwan. I'm someone who enjoys exploring different ideas, learning new things, and expressing creativity through various forms of media and storytelling.

Outside my studies, I spend much of my time gaming, reading, and writing. I particularly enjoy developing fictional worlds, creating characters, and coming up with plots and stories. I'm also interested in anime, VTubers, history, and different forms of fiction. These interests often inspire me to explore new ideas and perspectives.

I'm multilingual and enjoy learning languages. I speak Mandarin, English, and Burmese, and I'm currently teaching myself Japanese and German.

As an AI student, one of my biggest interests is exploring the relationship between artificial intelligence and human creativity. I believe AI should serve as a tool that helps creators develop their ideas, rather than something that replaces their imagination or creative abilities.

I'm still learning, experimenting, and discovering what I can accomplish. This website is a small collection of my interests, abilities, projects, and aspirations.

繁體中文

你好！我是 Xie-049，目前就讀於台灣亞洲大學人工智慧學系三年級。我喜歡探索不同想法、學習新事物，也喜歡透過各種媒體形式與說故事來表達創意。

課業之外，我常花時間玩遊戲、閱讀和寫作。我特別喜歡建構虛構世界、創作角色，以及發想情節與故事。我也對動畫、VTuber、歷史和各類虛構作品感興趣。這些興趣常帶領我探索新的想法與觀點。

我會說華語、英語和緬甸語，也很喜歡學習語言。目前我正在自學日語和德語。

身為人工智慧領域的學生，我很感興趣的一件事，是探索人工智慧與人類創意之間的關係。我相信 AI 應該成為協助創作者發展想法的工具，而不是取代創作者的想像力與創造能力。

我仍在學習、嘗試，並探索自己能做到什麼。這個網站整理了我的興趣、能力、創作計畫與未來期許。

06 / FUTURE GOALS 未來目標

Keeping
creativity human.

Curiosity about what AI can make possible.
持續探索 AI 能帶來的可能性。

“AI should empower creativity,
not replace it.”「AI 應該賦能創意，而不是取而代之。」
ENGLISH

I believe artificial intelligence should empower creativity, not replace it.

AI has opened up countless possibilities for creators, but excessive reliance on it can also discourage independent thinking and original ideas. I want to explore how AI can become a useful creative assistant: helping with research, brainstorming, development and experimentation, while leaving the imagination, decisions and creative direction in the hands of the creator.

To me, the creator should remain the brain behind the work, while AI serves as an assistant rather than a replacement.

繁體中文

我相信人工智慧應該促進創意，而不是取代創意。

AI 為創作者開啟了許多可能性，但過度依賴也可能讓人減少獨立思考與原創發想。我希望探索如何讓 AI 成為實用的創作助手：協助研究、腦力激盪、發展與實驗，同時讓想像、決策與創作方向仍由創作者掌握。

對我而言，創作者應該是作品背後的大腦，而 AI 則是助手，而不是替代者。
